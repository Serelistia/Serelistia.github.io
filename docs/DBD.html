DIGIT 210 Project: Dead By Daylight

Killer Lore Analysis

Spring 2026

Introduction to the game

Dead By Daylight is an asymmetrical 1v4 game where a group of four Survivors must repair five generators in order to escape, while avoiding a Killer whose goal is to hunt them down and sacrifice them to the Entity by hanging them from hooks.

Introduction to the project

For this project, I wanted to analyse the lore of each Killer currently in the game. The analysis focuses on frequency of words per Killer's lore and across all Killers' lores, seeing whether each Killer serves the Entity willingly, by force, by manipulation, or otherwise, and common themes (if any) between each Killer's lore.
